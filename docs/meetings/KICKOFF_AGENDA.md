# Kickoff: 30-minute game planning agenda

This follows the separate 30-minute ChatGPT study-workspace workshop in the first half of the hour. Micah operates the repository from his laptop; everyone can participate without accounts or development tools. Meeting date: TBD.

| Time | Activity and output |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 minutes | Introduce the repository, priorities, target timeframe, and small single-player boundary. Explain how proposals differ from decisions. |
| 3–10 minutes | Brief game pitches, aiming for about 30–45 seconds each. Describe the player goal and one main interaction; capture pitches as proposals. |
| 10–17 minutes | Narrow the options by feasibility, interest, and learning value. Seek explicit agreement on a direction if possible; record remaining alternatives and questions if not. |
| 17–23 minutes | Sketch the smallest playable version: core loop, player goal, one main mechanic, and what to leave out. Do not assume an engine or promise features. |
| 23–27 minutes | Identify unresolved questions, concept-dependent engine research, communication preferences, and volunteer planning tasks. Record owners/dates only when accepted. |
| 27–30 minutes | Read back decisions, proposals, open questions, and action items separately. Confirm the next milestone: a reviewed game plan by the next meeting, not necessarily a prototype. |

Use [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) for notes and [INITIAL_TASKS.md](../INITIAL_TASKS.md) for planning drafts. If recording is proposed, obtain participants' knowledge and agreement before it starts and follow the [manual summary workflow](../COMMUNICATION.md).

Questions to keep the scope small:

- What does the player do repeatedly, and why is that enjoyable?
- What is the smallest complete experience that would test the idea?
- Could we test the main interaction in one small scene or level?
- What can we cut before adding more systems, content, or volunteer hours?
- Can people contribute through writing, art, music, research, or testing?
- Which unknowns need answers before choosing an engine?
- Can this fit flexible participation and approximately 1–2 meetings per month?

If agreement needs more discussion, leave the concept open and choose a small follow-up planning task. The Minecraft-style magic example is not a preselected concept; multiplayer is outside initial scope.
