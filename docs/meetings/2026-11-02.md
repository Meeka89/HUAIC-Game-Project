# Game planning meeting summary

**Date:** November 2, 2026. **Source:** Meeting information supplied by Micah Kelley and his explicit confirmation of NPC interaction in the first prototype. Micah requested publication of this summary. Attendees, a vote, and separate review by other members have not been recorded. This is a concise project record, not a raw transcript.

## Agreed decisions

The first prototype includes one room with boundaries, a player who can walk around, and one NPC. An interaction button opens a short conversation; the player can close it and resume moving. Placeholder art is sufficient.

**Done when:** Another person can launch it, walk around, talk to the NPC, close the dialogue, and continue moving without getting stuck. Recorded as [D001](../DECISIONS.md#d001-first-prototype-scope); details in [PROTOTYPE.md](../PROTOTYPE.md).

This confirms prototype scope, not an engine selection, implementation assignment, or complete game plan.

## Proposed ideas and leading direction

The group is leaning toward a **Stardew Valley-inspired fantasy college-town game**. The player could work through semesters, try to graduate, and help save the world. Dragons, bisons, and Harding-inspired Easter eggs were raised, with a possibly medieval setting and a storyline.

Different teachers and other students could reveal information through interactions. Different classes could teach different information that helps defeat dragons and progress the game. These conversations and lessons would also build the world's lore. Exact mechanics remain proposals.

Visual possibilities include a mix of 2D and 3D and perhaps an 8-bit / retro pixel-art look. Animations were mentioned without a specific scope.

Other options raised earlier were roguelike games, exploring Harding University, and incremental games. They are discussion history, not approved features to combine with the leading concept.
